title: "Absence of spurious solutions far from ground truth: A low-rank analysis with high-order losses"
collection: publications

date: 2023-10-17
venue: 'International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics(AISTATS) 2024, <span style="font-weight: bold;">Oral presentation</span>'
paperurl: '/files/papers/aistats24.pdf'
abstract: >-
  We analyze matrix-sensing landscapes far from the ground truth, where spurious solutions can obstruct local optimization. Under suitable conditions, distant critical points are strict saddles rather than local minima, and higher-order losses amplify the negative curvature around them. This provides a theoretical alternative to increasing model complexity through overparametrization.
