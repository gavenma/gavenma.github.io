title: "Sharp Restricted Isometry Property Bounds for Low-rank Matrix Recovery Problems with Corrupted Measurements"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'Ziye Ma<sup>*</sup>, Yingjie Bi<sup>*</sup>, Javad Lavaei, Somayeh Sojoudi'
date: 2022-02-01
venue: '36th AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence 2022.'
paperurl: 'https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08232.pdf'
abstract: >-
  We study low-rank matrix recovery with noisy linear measurements and ask when local search can find the ground truth with small error. We derive sharp guarantees for local minimizers under a restricted-isometry assumption, show how the guarantee improves as noise decreases, and prove a strict-saddle property that supports polynomial-time convergence of perturbed gradient descent.
