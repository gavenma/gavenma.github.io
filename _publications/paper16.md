date: 2025-05-20
venue: 'Preprint.'
paperurl: '/files/papers/Matrix_Lasso_NeurIPS_2025.pdf'
abstract: >-
  We study matrix sensing through the convex matrix-lasso relaxation, where the nuclear norm replaces rank. We show that the relaxation can certifiably recover a low-rank ground-truth matrix and establish a connection to its Burer--Monteiro factorization. The guarantees extend to finite-variance noise and clarify the relationship between convex and non-convex formulations.
