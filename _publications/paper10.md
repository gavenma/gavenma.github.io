title: "Algorithmic Regularization in Tensor Optimization: Towards a Lifted Approach in Matrix Sensing"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'Ziye Ma, Javad Lavaei, Somayeh Sojoudi'
date: 2023-05-18
venue: 'Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 2023.'
paperurl: 'https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.15549.pdf'
abstract: >-
  We examine how gradient descent induces implicit regularization in tensor optimization through a lifted matrix-sensing framework. With sufficiently small initialization, gradient descent produces approximately rank-one tensors and critical points with escape directions. The results highlight how tensor parametrization and first-order methods can work together to reach global optima.
