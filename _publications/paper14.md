title: "Topology-Aware 3D Gaussian Splatting: Leveraging Persistent Homology for Optimized Structural Integrity"

date: 2024-12-10
venue: '39th AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence 2025, <span style="font-weight: bold;">Oral presentation</span>'
paperurl: 'https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.16619'
abstract: >-
  We introduce Topology-Aware 3D Gaussian Splatting, combining local persistent Voronoi interpolation with a persistent-homology regularizer. The method improves geometric coverage and preserves topological structure during optimization. Experiments across novel-view-synthesis benchmarks improve PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS while maintaining efficient memory use.
