title: "Geometric Analysis of Noisy Low-rank Matrix Recovery in the Exact Parameterized and the Overparameterized Regimes"
collection: publications

date: 2021-10-22
venue: 'INFORMS Journal on Optimization'
paperurl: 'https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/pdf/10.1287/ijoo.2023.0090'
abstract: >-
  We study noisy matrix sensing in both exact-parametrization and overparametrization regimes. The analysis quantifies how the restricted-isometry property shapes the non-convex landscape, bounds the distance of local minimizers from the ground truth, and proves a strict-saddle property that yields global convergence of perturbed gradient descent.
