venue: '2020 59th IEEE Conference on Decision and Control (CDC)'
paperurl: 'https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.00570.pdf'
abstract: >-
  We study certification of neural-network robustness to perturbed and adversarial inputs. A partition-based procedure exploits the structure of ReLU networks to tighten existing linear-programming relaxations, reaching zero relaxation error with a finite partition. Experiments show that the method can issue robustness certificates where prior approaches fail.
