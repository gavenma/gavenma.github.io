title: "Over-parametrization via Lifting for Low-rank Matrix Sensing: Conversion of Spurious Solutions to Strict Saddle Points"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'Ziye Ma, Igor Molybog, Javad Lavaei, Somayeh Sojoudi'
date: 2023-01-28
venue: 'International Conference on Machine Learning(ICML) 2023, <span style="font-weight: bold;">Oral presentation</span>'
paperurl: 'https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ma23f.html'
abstract: >-
  We study over-parametrization for low-rank matrix sensing by constructing an infinite hierarchy of lifted non-convex problems through tensor lifting and Burer--Monteiro factorization. Spurious solutions remain stationary through the hierarchy but become strict saddle points under technical conditions, allowing local search methods to escape them. We also derive a bound on how much over-parametrization is needed to eliminate spurious solutions.
