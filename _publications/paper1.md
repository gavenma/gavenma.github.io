venue: 'IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters'
paperurl: 'https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.03554.pdf'
abstract: >-
  We present a certifiably globally optimal algorithm for determining the extrinsic calibration between two sensors that can produce independent egomotion estimates. The problem is formulated as a quadratically constrained quadratic program and solved through a semidefinite-programming relaxation with a global optimality certificate. The method is fast, robust to noise, and does not require a prior estimate or operator intervention.
